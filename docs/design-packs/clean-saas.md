# Clean SaaS Product

## Purpose

This design pack gives product repos a trustworthy, product-led starting point for:
- SaaS apps
- customer-facing dashboards
- onboarding surfaces
- product websites that should feel modern without feeling flashy

It is meant to feel clear, credible, and quietly polished.

## Best Fit

Use this pack when the product needs to feel:
- reliable
- modern
- efficient
- calm enough for daily use

It fits especially well when:
- the repo has both product UI and a light marketing surface
- the brand should feel polished but not luxury-heavy
- the team wants fewer one-off UI choices

## Design Intent

- audience: founders, operators, and product users who value clarity over novelty
- brand feel: trustworthy, clean, practical
- product posture: capable and mature, even if the product is still early
- trust level: high clarity, low drama

## Visual Foundations

- color direction: restrained neutrals with one confident primary accent
- typography direction: highly legible sans-serif with strong hierarchy and moderate contrast
- spacing and rhythm: generous enough to breathe, but not airy to the point of waste
- component tone: soft corners, clear states, minimal ornament

## Surface Rules

- app surfaces should prioritize information hierarchy and task completion
- marketing surfaces should feel consistent with the product rather than like a separate brand universe
- motion should be light and purposeful
- icons and illustrations should be simple and low-noise

## Design Guardrails

- avoid over-styling routine screens
- avoid trend-heavy gradients or decorative motion by default
- do not let the landing surface become visually louder than the product itself
- keep accessibility and readability ahead of aesthetic novelty

## Good Starting Uses

- B2B SaaS product
- internal tool with a customer-facing layer
- founder-built product that needs credibility quickly

## Poor Fit

- highly expressive consumer brands
- luxury or editorial-first brands
- gaming or entertainment-heavy visual systems
