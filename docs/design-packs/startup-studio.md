# Startup Studio / Founder Service

## Purpose

This design pack gives startup studios, founder services, and operator-led product surfaces a stronger starting point for:
- advisory websites
- productized service pages
- founder-facing onboarding surfaces
- hybrid company-plus-product brands

It should feel strategic, warm, and high-trust without becoming stiff.

## Best Fit

Use this pack when the repo needs to feel:
- founder-friendly
- credible
- premium but approachable
- more strategic than purely technical

It fits especially well when:
- the same brand spans service, product, and thought-leadership surfaces
- trust and clarity matter more than aggressive conversion tropes
- the repo needs a brand system that can support both guidance and execution

## Design Intent

- audience: founders, small startup teams, and operators
- brand feel: thoughtful, confident, human
- product posture: a partner with strong judgment, not just a vendor
- trust level: warm expertise

## Visual Foundations

- color direction: soft neutrals with a controlled premium accent range
- typography direction: expressive but disciplined, with a human tone
- spacing and rhythm: roomy, editorial, and calm
- component tone: cards, sections, and calls to action should feel composed rather than salesy

## Brand And Interaction Direction

- marketing surfaces should feel more like strong editorial guidance than hype-heavy funnels
- app or workflow surfaces should preserve the same trust and clarity
- motion should support pacing and emphasis rather than visual spectacle
- visual texture can be slightly richer than a SaaS baseline, but still restrained

## Design Guardrails

- avoid startup cliches like empty “future of work” hero language and generic gradient theater
- avoid cold enterprise stiffness
- do not mix too many visual metaphors across service and product surfaces
- keep the brand warm without becoming casual or inconsistent

## Good Starting Uses

- startup studio sites
- founder operating partners
- AI product studios
- service businesses with a structured delivery workflow

## Poor Fit

- very technical operator consoles
- highly transactional ecommerce surfaces
- entertainment-first consumer brands
