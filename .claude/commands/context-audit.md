# /context-audit

## Purpose

Audit whether the repo context is healthy, aligned, and lean enough to support good orchestration.

## Owned By

- Lead: `@master` or `@tech-writer`
- Related skill: `context-audit`

## What It Should Do

1. inspect briefing quality and doc alignment
2. identify drift, gaps, or bloat
3. recommend cleanup or strengthening work

## Output

- context-audit report
- recommended fixes
