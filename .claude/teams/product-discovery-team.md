# Product Discovery Team

## Purpose

Default team for turning an early idea into a credible product direction, MVP shape, and execution-ready backlog before implementation starts.

This team is the default home for MVP reduction work before a repo jumps into implementation too early.

## Lead

- `@product-owner` for scope, sequencing, and acceptance-shape work
- `@vision-partner` for collaborative direction-shaping when the user still needs stronger options before narrowing

## Supporting Agents

- `@business-analyst`
- `@product-designer`
- `@customer-advocate`
- `@idea-executor`
- `@strategy-reviewer`
- `@session-budget-estimator`

## Typical Triggers

- "I have an idea for an app or website"
- MVP framing and scope reduction
- audience, use-case, or problem-definition work
- requirements, backlog, or roadmap shaping before build
- deciding what is necessary now versus later

## Default Flow

- clarify the user, problem, and intended outcome
- reduce the idea into a realistic first product shape
- identify the smallest credible MVP, proof, or launch surface
- turn the result into backlog, roadmap, or implementation-ready next steps
- surface what should not be built yet

## Required Gates

- `@strategy-reviewer` before treating a broad new capability or expansion path as a strong next move
- `@session-budget-estimator` when the main risk is that the plan is too large for one realistic implementation arc
