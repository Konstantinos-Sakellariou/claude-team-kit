# AI/ML Team

## Purpose

Default team for model-centric work spanning exploratory analysis, training, evaluation, rollout readiness, and research-heavy ML decisions.

## Lead

- `@data-scientist` for framing, exploratory work, and baseline design
- `@ml-engineer` for training and pipeline implementation

## Supporting Agents

- `@model-evaluator`
- `@mlops-engineer`
- `@research-scientist`

## Typical Triggers

- exploratory analysis
- model training
- evaluation and fairness review
- model deployment planning
- literature review or benchmark critique

## Default Flow

- problem framing and baselines
- model implementation
- mandatory evaluation gate
- rollout and monitoring planning
- optional research support for novel methods

## Required Gates

- `@model-evaluator` before deployment-minded or production-readiness conclusions
