# Research & Discovery Team

## Purpose

Default team for external repo reviews, tool evaluations, ecosystem scans, image/reference fit reviews, and source-backed discovery work that should inform a decision instead of staying as loose inspiration.

## Lead

- `@researcher` for evidence gathering, synthesis, and source-backed judgment
- `@vision-partner` when the main need is not only facts, but stronger option generation or fit interpretation

## Supporting Agents

- `@strategy-reviewer`
- `@tech-writer`
- `@backlog-updater`
- `@source-verifier`

## Typical Triggers

- external repo fit review
- library, tool, or workflow evaluation
- ecosystem scan or market-map review
- image, design, brand, or prompt reference evaluation
- "what should we borrow from this?"
- "is this a good fit for our repo?"

## Default Flow

- clarify which decision the research should inform
- review the source or reference with a fit lens, not a fandom lens
- separate documented fact from interpretation
- classify the result as strong fit, partial fit, inspiration only, or poor fit
- capture the memo locally under `.claude/local-context/research/` when the result should persist
- recommend backlog or plan follow-up only when the finding deserves real work

## Required Gates

- `@strategy-reviewer` before treating a new external influence as a strong roadmap move
- `@backlog-updater` when the outcome becomes deferred work worth saving
