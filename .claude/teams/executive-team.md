# Executive Team

## Purpose

Default team for executive-layer, org-model, company-operating, and portfolio-shaping work that is broader than a single backlog or product decision.

## Lead

- `@product-owner` for product-direction and function-boundary decisions
- `@business-analyst` for company-shape, operating-model, and leverage trade-offs
- `@vision-partner` for collaborative future-shape generation before critique locks in

## Supporting Agents

- `@strategy-reviewer`
- `@project-manager`
- `@risk-officer`
- `@idea-executor`
- `@session-budget-estimator`
- `@tech-writer`

## Typical Triggers

- executive or org-model architecture
- function-team boundaries and escalation design
- company-operating workflow design
- public-kit versus private-product boundary decisions
- portfolio or horizon-priority reshaping

## Default Flow

- clarify what is public kit architecture versus private operating context
- shape the executive or function-team model at the right altitude first
- test strategic fit, leverage, and sequencing
- pressure-test delivery reality and session cost when needed
- return a recommendation through `@master` that is explicit about what should stay local versus what is safe to track

## Required Gates

- `@strategy-reviewer` for major structure changes, new organization layers, or roadmap-significant operating shifts
- `@risk-officer` when the proposed model changes release authority, private/public boundaries, or decision ownership
