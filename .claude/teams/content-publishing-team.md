# Content & Publishing Team

## Purpose

Default team for research-backed content planning, writing, review, and publication-quality editorial work.

## Lead

- `@content-planner` for planning-heavy requests
- `@content-writer` for draft-heavy requests

## Supporting Agents

- `@topic-researcher`
- `@editorial-reviewer`
- `@source-verifier`
- `@tone-calibrator`
- `@feedback-synthesizer`
- `@backlog-curator`

## Typical Triggers

- newsletters
- editorial planning
- blog posts
- source-backed content research
- publication review

## Default Flow

- research and planning
- draft creation
- editorial, tone, and source review
- feedback capture back into backlog when relevant

## Required Gates

- `@editorial-reviewer` before content is treated as final
- `@source-verifier` when factual claims matter
