# Advisory Review Team

## Purpose

Default team for scope, decision-making, business, product, UX, risk, and strategic validation work.

## Lead

- `@product-owner` for scope/product decisions
- `@business-analyst` for ROI/requirements decisions
- `@idea-executor` for idea-to-plan work

## Supporting Agents

- `@project-manager`
- `@customer-advocate`
- `@devils-advocate`
- `@risk-officer`
- `@judge`
- `@tech-writer`

## Typical Triggers

- idea shaping
- product or business evaluation
- prioritization decisions
- trade-off analysis
- risk-heavy or contested choices

## Default Flow

- frame the decision
- challenge assumptions
- evaluate consequences
- document the outcome when durable

## Required Gates

- `@devils-advocate` for significant decisions
- `@judge` when competing options need a final verdict
